## What it is

The movement of cash and bank accounts over a period, shown as money in and money out by account, with the opening and closing balance.

## When to use it

To explain why cash went up or down compared with profit.

## Good to know

[[financial-statements]] also has a cash-flow statement in the indirect method (starting from profit).
