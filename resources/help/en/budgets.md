## What it is

A **budget** plans income and expenses by account (and optionally cost center) and month. **Budget against actual** then compares it with what really happened.

## When to use it

Before the year starts (to plan), and every month (to follow).

## How to

1. **New budget**: pick the first and last month and add a line per account, with an annual figure that is spread evenly or amounts by month. Or **build from last year** with a percentage change, or **copy** a budget to a later year.
2. The accountant plans; an approver **approves** it (only one approved budget may cover a month). Close it at the end.
3. Open **Budget vs actual** for the whole budget or any range of months. Each row shows budget, actual, variance and a status: *ok*, *warning*, *over*, *behind* or *unbudgeted*.

## Good to know

Your administrator can make the system refuse a posting that takes a budgeted expense over its budget; people with the override permission can still post.
