## What it is

Instead of retyping the bank's statement, upload it (CSV or Excel). The system reads the lines, matches them to what you already recorded, helps you book what is missing, and reconciles.

## When to use it

Once a month per bank account, or whenever you want to check the bank against your books.

## How to

1. **Import**: choose the bank account and the file. Columns are recognised by name (Date, Description, Reference, Debit/Withdrawal, Credit/Deposit or Amount, Balance). A **preview** shows every row first; if any row has an error nothing is imported.
2. **Auto-match**: lines that have exactly one matching ledger entry (same amount, within a few days) are matched for you.
3. The rest: **match by hand** from the list of candidates, **book** a line that is not in the books yet (bank charges, interest, a direct deposit), or **ignore** it.
4. When every line is matched, booked or ignored, **Reconcile**.

## Good to know

- Importing an overlapping statement again brings in only the new lines; nothing is duplicated.
- A reconciled statement is locked.
