## What it is

A tax code is a named tax such as *Sales tax 18%*. It has a **kind** — *output* (collected on sales), *input* (paid on purchases), *withheld* or *advance* — the **tax account** it is booked to, and a jurisdiction.

## When to use it

Before you raise taxed invoices or bills.

## Good to know

The rate itself is kept in [[tax-rates]], with dates, so old documents keep the rate of their day.
