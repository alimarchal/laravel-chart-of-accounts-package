## What it is

Four related screens: **Loans & advances** (lent to employees, recovered from their salary), **Arrears** (back pay when a raise is applied late), **Bonuses** (one-off earnings or deductions) and **Settlements** (what is paid when someone leaves).

## When to use it

- A loan is requested, an advance given, a raise applied late, a bonus or fine decided, an employee leaving.

## How to

- **Loan**: enter the amount, the number of instalments and the first salary it comes out of; then **pay it out**. Each payroll run takes the due instalment automatically; you can **skip** one or **settle** the rest in cash.
- **Arrears**: **preview** the difference between what was paid and what the salary history says was due, create it, have it **approved**; the next payroll run pays it.
- **Bonuses**: for one employee and month, or for everyone as a fixed amount or percent; the month's run takes it up.
- **Settlements**: preview the gratuity, unused leave pay and loans still owed, then post and pay.

## Good to know

A voided or deleted run gives instalments, arrears and bonuses back, so nothing is lost or counted twice.
