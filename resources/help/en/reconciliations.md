## What it is

A reconciliation says: *on this date, the bank's closing balance and our books agreed*, and lists the entries that were cleared. Reconciling a bank statement creates one.

## When to use it

To see what has been reconciled and when, and to check that a bank account is up to date before closing a period (the close checklist warns about unreconciled bank lines).
