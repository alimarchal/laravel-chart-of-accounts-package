## What it is

Everything beyond the core (chart, journal, periods, reports, users) is optional. Here an administrator turns modules (inventory, fixed assets, budgets, payroll, WhatsApp …) and payroll features (loans, arrears, attendance …) on and off.

## When to use it

If your business does not use a module and you want it out of the way.

## Good to know

- An off module disappears from the menus and its pages answer *not found*; nothing is deleted, and turning it on again brings everything back.
- A payroll sub-feature is off whenever payroll itself is off.
- Every change is in the audit log.
