## What it is

A one-page summary of the company: cash and bank, what customers owe you, what you owe suppliers, income against expense for the last six months, the year to date, the biggest expenses, recent entries and a list of **things that need attention**.

## When to use it

Open it first thing in the morning, or before a meeting. Every number links to the screen behind it, so you can go from "payables look high" to the list of bills in one click.

## How to read it

- **Needs attention** — draft entries, entries waiting for approval, overdue invoices and so on. Each line takes you to the screen that fixes it.
- **Receivables and payables ageing** — how late the money is. *Ledger vs sub-ledger difference* should be zero; if it is not, someone posted by hand to a control account (see [[control-accounts]]).
- **As of date** — use the date box at the top to see the picture on any earlier day.

## Good to know

- You see only the sections your role allows.
- With branches switched on, the figures follow the branch you chose in the switcher.
