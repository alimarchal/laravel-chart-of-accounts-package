## What it is

Several places of business inside one company and one chart of accounts. Branches are optional: nothing changes until they are switched on.

## When to use it

If you want entries, invoices, payments and staff tagged by place, and reports per place.

## How to

1. Create the branches here.
2. Give each person the branches they work in (on the user, or on the branch). People with no branch restriction, and admins, see all.
3. Pick the **working branch** in the Branch switcher: new records belong to it and reports follow it.

## Good to know

- A branch with entries cannot be deleted; deactivate it.
- Period close and the year-end close are company-wide.
- For separate books (a different legal entity) use multi-company instead.
