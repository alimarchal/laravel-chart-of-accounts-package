---
## What it is

A label you can put on journal lines (and on employees, budgets and so on) to see income and cost per department, project or class.

## When to use it

If management asks "what did the transport department cost?". If not, you can ignore cost centers.

## How to

Create the cost centers here; then choose one on the lines of an entry, on an employee, or on a budget line. Reports can be filtered by cost center.
