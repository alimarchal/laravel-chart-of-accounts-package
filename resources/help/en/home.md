## What it is

The home page of the accounting area: one card for every screen your role allows, grouped like the menu (journal and ledger, financial reports, ageing and banking, bank and reconciliation), with counts where they help.

## When to use it

When you are not sure where something is. Browsing the cards is also the quickest way to see what the system can do. The **Settings** tab at the top opens the same kind of page for users, roles and the set-up of the books.

## Good to know

- Cards for switched-off modules are hidden (see [[features]]).
- Everything on this page is also in the menu.
