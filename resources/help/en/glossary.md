## Words you will meet

**Account** — a named bucket for money: *Cash in hand*, *Salary expense*, *Customer fees*. Every amount you record goes into accounts.

**Chart of accounts** — the list of all your accounts, arranged in groups. See [[chart-of-accounts]].

**Debit and credit** — the two sides of every entry. Cash coming in is a debit to *Cash* and a credit to the account that explains why (for example *Fee income*). Every entry must have equal debits and credits; that is what *balanced* means.

**Journal entry (voucher)** — one record of something that happened, with two or more lines. See [[journal-entries]].

**Draft and posted** — a draft can still be changed; a **posted** entry is in the books for good (it can only be reversed). Posting gives it its voucher number.

**Reverse and void** — *reverse* posts a mirror image of a posted entry to cancel it; *void* cancels a draft that was never posted.

**Period** — a month or a year in which entries are allowed. A **closed** period accepts nothing more. See [[periods]].

**Trial balance** — a list of every account with its debit and credit totals; the two sides must be equal. See [[trial-balance]].

**Base currency** — the currency of your books. Entries in another currency are converted to it at the rate you give.

**Control account** — an account that summarises a list of people or things (all customers, all suppliers), so only the module that owns the list may post to it. See [[control-accounts]].

**Cost center** — a department or project you want to see costs for. See [[cost-centers]].

**Reconciliation** — checking that the bank's statement and your books agree. See [[reconciliations]].

**Maker–checker** — when one person records and another approves, so nobody approves their own work.
