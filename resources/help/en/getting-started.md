## How the menu is organised

Everything lives under one **Accounting** entry in your menu. It opens into sections:

| Section | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The dashboard (cash, receivables, payables, what needs attention) and *All modules*, a page with a card for every screen |
| **Journal & ledger** | Journal entries, recurring entries, the chart of accounts, account types, currency revaluation, balance snapshots |
| **Sales & purchases** | Customers and suppliers, invoices and bills, receipts and payments, ageing, tax, FBR, WhatsApp |
| **Banking** | Bank accounts, bank statements, reconciliations, bank book, cash book |
| **Payroll** | Salary runs, employees, attendance and leave, loans, payroll reports |
| **Assets & stock** | Fixed assets and inventory |
| **Reports** | Trial balance, general ledger, balance sheet, income statement, cash flow, financial statements, budgets |
| **Settings** | Users and access, then the set-up of the books (periods, currencies, cost centers, branches, voucher types, tax, control accounts, modules on and off) |

You only see what your role allows, and a module that has been switched off disappears from the menu.

## A good order for a new company

1. **Settings → Accounting periods**: create the fiscal year (or its twelve months). Nothing can be posted outside an open period.
2. **Chart of accounts**: check the accounts you start with, or apply an industry template.
3. **Settings → Account roles**: tell the system which account is your cash, your bank, your receivables.
4. **Settings → Users**: give each person a role (see [[roles]]).
5. Start working: [[journal-entries]], [[parties]] and [[party-documents]], [[bank-statements]], [[payroll]].
6. Look at the numbers: [[overview]], [[trial-balance]], [[balance-sheet]], [[income-statement]].
7. At month end: close the period (see [[periods]]).

## Help on every screen

Each screen has a **?** button (a *Help* button at the bottom right in the React screens) that opens the guide of the screen you are on. The [[glossary]] explains the accounting words in plain language, and [[shortcuts]] lists the quick ways to work.
