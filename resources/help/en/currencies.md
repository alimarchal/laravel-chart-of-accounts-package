## What it is

The list of currencies, one of which is the **base currency** of your books, with the exchange rate of each to it.

## When to use it

When you start dealing in a foreign currency, or to update a rate.

## Good to know

- Each entry stores its own currency and rate; when it is posted, the base amounts are frozen, so a later rate change never changes history.
- Closing-rate revaluation is on its own screen.
