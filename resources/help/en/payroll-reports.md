## What it is

Reports from the posted payroll: **month by month** (with the change from the previous month), **cost by cost center**, and **headcount**; and the **salary tax statement** of a tax year with a printable **tax certificate** for each employee.

## When to use it

For management's monthly view of staff cost, and at tax-year end for the tax certificates.

## How to

Choose the year (and months) at the top. Use the **CSV / XLSX / PDF** buttons to export. On the tax screen pick the tax year and an employee to open the certificate.

## Good to know

The tax year starts in the month set in the configuration (July by default).
