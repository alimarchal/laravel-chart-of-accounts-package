## What it is

Your accounts arranged as a tree: *Assets* contains *Current assets*, which contains *Cash in hand* and *Bank accounts*, and so on. **Group** accounts hold other accounts; **posting** accounts are the ones entries are made to.

## When to use it

When you need a new account, want to find an account's code, or need to tidy the list. A fresh installation starts with a complete standard chart.

## How to

- **Add account**: give a code and a name, pick the parent group and the account type.
- **Tree view**: see the accounts as a tree; expand and collapse groups.
- **Templates**: add the accounts an industry usually needs (trading, manufacturing, services, school, NGO, healthcare). Only missing accounts are added; nothing you have is changed.
- **Import / Export**: bring a chart in from Excel or CSV (a preview shows what would change first), or export it.
- The ⇄ icon on an account **renumbers** it, or **merges** a duplicate into another account.

## Rules that protect you

- An account can have a parent only if the parent is a group of the same type.
- Once an account has entries, its code, type and normal balance can no longer be changed (rename or deactivate it instead).
- An account with entries or sub-accounts cannot be deleted.
