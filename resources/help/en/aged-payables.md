## What it is

The same report as [[aged-receivables]], for supplier bills: not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days past due.

## When to use it

To plan the week's payments and to avoid paying late (or too early).

## Good to know

Unapplied payments and debit notes reduce what is owed. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.
