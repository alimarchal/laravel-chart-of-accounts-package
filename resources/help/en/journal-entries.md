## What it is

The list of all vouchers. Each entry has a date, a voucher type, a reference, a description and **lines**: each line puts an amount on the debit or credit side of an account. An entry is **balanced** when the debits equal the credits; only balanced entries can be saved.

## When to use it

For anything that happened and is not recorded by another module: an expense paid, a fee received, a transfer between accounts, a correction. (Invoices, receipts, salaries and depreciation are recorded by their own screens, which create the entries for you.)

## How to record an entry

1. Click **New entry**.
2. Fill the header: date, voucher type (*Journal*, *Cash payment*, *Bank receipt* …), reference, description. The currency is your base currency unless you change it.
3. Add lines: pick an account (type its number or name), then type the amount once, in *Debit* or in *Credit*. Add a cost center if you track one.
4. Watch the bar at the bottom: **Balanced** means you can save. Use **Balance** on a line to fill in what is missing.
5. **Save** keeps it as a draft; **Post** puts it in the books and gives it its voucher number.

## Good to know

- **Draft** can be edited (open it and click *Edit draft*) or voided. **Posted** can only be **reversed**: the reversal is a new entry that cancels it, numbered in the same series.
- A posted entry belongs to the accounting period of its date; if that period is closed it is refused.
- With *maker–checker* switched on, large entries are **submitted for approval** instead of posted: an approver then approves or rejects them (see [[roles]]).
- Attach the supporting bill or receipt to the entry (*Supporting documents*). Above a certain amount the system may require one.
- The *Document no.* of a bill can be posted only once, so the same bill cannot be booked twice.
