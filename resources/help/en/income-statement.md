title: Income statement
summary: Income and expenses over a period, and the profit or loss.
---
## What it is

Revenue minus expenses for the period you choose. Pick the **from** and **to** dates in the filters.

## When to use it

To see whether the business made money this month or this year.

## Good to know

Year-end closing entries are left out, so the statement of a closed year still shows its income and expenses. With branches on, the figures follow the chosen branch.
