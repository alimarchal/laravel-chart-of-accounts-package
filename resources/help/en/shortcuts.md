---
title: Shortcuts and quick ways to work
summary: Keyboard and mouse tricks that save time.
---
## In every list

- **Filters** opens the filter panel. When something is filtered you see chips with what is applied and a **Clear all** link. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close the panel.
- Dropdowns are searchable: open one and just start typing.
- The page buttons at the top right: **Filters**, refresh, print, and the **?** that opens this help.

## In the journal entry form

- Choose an account by typing its number or part of its name.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> on a Mac) saves the entry.
- The **Balance** button on a line puts the amount that is still missing on that line, so debits equal credits.
- A new line starts with what is still missing, so the last line is usually just a click.
- The bar at the bottom always shows *Balanced* or *Out of balance by …*.

## Anywhere

- The **Branch** and **Company** switchers (when your installation uses them) decide whose data you are looking at.
- The language switcher changes the language of the screens; numbers and your data do not change.
