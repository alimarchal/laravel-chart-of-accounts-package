## What it is

Entries can only be posted inside an **open period**. Periods are usually twelve monthly ones per fiscal year.

## When to use it

At the start of each year (create the periods) and at the end of each month and year (close them).

## How to

1. **Monthly periods**: enter the fiscal year's first day to create all twelve.
2. **Month-end close**: opens a **checklist**: no entries waiting for approval, no drafts dated in the period, trial balance balanced, earlier periods closed, bank lines reconciled. Each item links to the screen that fixes it. Closing locks the period and stores the balance snapshots.
3. **Year-end close**: also posts the closing entry that moves the year's profit to retained earnings. You see a preview first.
4. **Reopen** (needs a reason, newest period first) if something must be corrected.

## Good to know

Periods cannot overlap, and their dates cannot change once they contain entries.
