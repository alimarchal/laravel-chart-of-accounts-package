## What it is

A monthly **attendance sheet** (absent days and overtime hours per employee) and **leave**: leave types (paid or unpaid, with a yearly entitlement), the leaves taken and the yearly balances.

## When to use it

Before calculating the month's payroll.

## How to

- **Attendance**: enter absent days (half days allowed) and overtime hours for the month.
- **Leave**: record a leave for an employee with its dates; the balance of the type is checked. Unpaid leave comes off the salary by the day.

## Good to know

- Employees flagged *Paid overtime* get hours × (salary ÷ hours per month) × the multiplier.
- A month whose payroll is already posted cannot be changed.
- Leave that overlaps other leave, or exceeds a paid type's balance, is refused.
