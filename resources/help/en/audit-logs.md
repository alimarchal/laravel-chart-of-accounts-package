## What it is

Every important action (posting, reversing, closing a period, changing a user or a setting, importing a chart …) is written to the audit log with the person, the time and the details. The log cannot be edited from the screens.

## When to use it

To answer "who changed this?" and for audits.

## How to

Use **Filters** (user, action, date). Open a row for its full details.

## Good to know

The trail is written even if this screen is switched off.
