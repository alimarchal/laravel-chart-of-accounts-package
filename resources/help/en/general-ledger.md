## What it is

The complete history of entries: date, account, description, voucher number, reference, debit and credit.

## When to use it

To see exactly how an account got its balance, or to find an entry from a clue (an amount, a document number).

## How to

**Filters**: account, dates, status, document number, cost center. Click an account for its **statement** with a running balance. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.
