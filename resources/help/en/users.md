## What it is

The list of people who can use the system. Each user has one or more **roles** (what they may do), optional direct permissions, and, if branches are used, the branches they work in.

## When to use it

When someone joins or leaves, or when their duties change.

## How to

1. **New user**: name, e-mail, password, role(s) and branches.
2. To change duties, edit the user and change the role.
3. **Permissions** on a user's page shows exactly what that person can do and lets an administrator give or take away single permissions.

## Good to know

- You can only give roles and permissions that you hold yourself; only a super-admin manages super-admins.
- Remove access by deleting the user or giving them no role.
