## What it is

The list of the bank accounts the company has. Each is tied to an account of the chart of accounts, so what you record against the bank shows in the ledger.

## When to use it

When you open a new bank account, or before you import a statement for it.

## Good to know

- Statements are imported and reconciled per bank account (see [[bank-statements]]).
- With branches switched on, a bank account belongs to a branch.
