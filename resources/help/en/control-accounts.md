## What it is

The *Accounts receivable* account must always equal the total of the customers' balances. To guarantee it, only the receivables module may post to it: a hand-made journal entry touching it is refused. The same applies to payables, inventory, fixed assets, payroll and tax.

## When to use it

Once at set-up: click **Recommended setup** to mark the standard accounts, or mark any posting account yourself.

## Good to know

- People with the *post manually to control accounts* permission can still adjust them; every such entry appears in the **Manual postings** list, which an auditor reviews first.
- Reversals and year-end closing entries are never blocked.
