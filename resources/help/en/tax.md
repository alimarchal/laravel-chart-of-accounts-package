## What it is

The tax screen brings together the **tax codes** with their current rate, a **calculator**, **taxed documents** (invoices, bills and payments with tax in one go), the **tax report** for any period, and the **returns** you have filed.

## When to use it

When you book a document with tax, and when it is time to settle the tax for a period.

## How to

1. Make sure the tax codes and rates are set up (see [[tax-codes]] and [[tax-rates]]).
2. **New taxed document** books an invoice, bill, credit or debit note, or a payment with tax withheld; a live preview shows the tax before you save.
3. **Tax report** shows, for each tax code, the taxable base, the tax and the number of documents in the period. Totals: output tax, input tax, **net payable**.
4. **File a return** settles a period in one entry: output tax against input tax, the difference to the account you pick. A return can be voided and the period filed again.

## Good to know

- Periods of two returns cannot overlap.
- Credit notes reduce the base and the tax.
