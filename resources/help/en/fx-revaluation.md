## What it is

If you hold a USD bank account or owe a supplier in euros, the balance in your base currency changes whenever the exchange rate does. At the end of a period this screen restates those balances at the closing rate; the difference is an **unrealised exchange gain or loss**.

## When to use it

At month end or year end, after all entries are in and before you close the period.

## How to

1. Pick the **date** and type the closing rate of each currency (or keep a dated rate history).
2. Click **Calculate**. The preview lists every foreign-currency account with its foreign balance, the rate, the book value, the revalued amount and the adjustment. Nothing is posted yet.
3. Choose the **gain/loss account** and, if you like, **auto-reverse** (the adjustment is reversed the next day, the usual month-end routine).
4. **Post** the revaluation: one adjusting entry is made.

## Good to know

- It is safe to repeat: running it again at the same rates adjusts nothing.
- Each run keeps its detail and can be reversed later from its own page.
