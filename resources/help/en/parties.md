## What it is

The list of **parties**: customers, suppliers, or both. Each one has payment terms (the default number of days until an invoice is due), an optional credit limit, a tax number and contact details. Their balances are tracked by the system, so you always know who owes you and whom you owe.

## When to use it

Before you can raise an invoice or a bill, the customer or supplier must exist here.

## How to

1. **New customer or supplier**: name, type, payment terms (and a credit limit if you want a warning when it is passed).
2. Open a party to see its **open items** (unpaid invoices with the days overdue), unapplied payments and a **statement of account** for any period with a running balance.

## Good to know

- Parties post to the *receivables* and *payables* control accounts, so those balances can only be moved by invoices, bills and payments, never by a hand-made entry (see [[control-accounts]]).
- A party with documents cannot be deleted; make it inactive instead.
- With branches switched on, a party belongs to the branch it was created in.
