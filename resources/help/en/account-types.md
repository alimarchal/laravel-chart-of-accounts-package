## What it is

The five families of accounts and the side each one normally grows on: assets and expenses grow on the debit side; liabilities, equity and income on the credit side. Reports use them to put every account in the right place.

## When to use it

Rarely. The five standard types are created for you. Open this screen only if your accountant wants an extra classification.

## Good to know

- Every account in the chart has exactly one type; its children must have the same type.
- Types and currencies are shared by all companies of the installation.
