## What it is

A **receipt** is money received from a customer; a **payment** is money paid to a supplier. Each is recorded against a bank or cash account and numbered (*RCT-…*, *PAY-…*).

## When to use it

When a customer pays an invoice or you pay a supplier's bill.

## How to

1. **New receipt / payment**: pick the party, the bank or cash account, the date and the amount.
2. **Apply** it to specific invoices (or bills), or leave it so the oldest due item is paid first. Any part not applied stays on the account as an *unapplied* payment.
3. Mistakes: a payment can be voided, and applications can be undone.

## Good to know

- Receipts and payments are posted at once; they cannot be saved as drafts.
- A tax amount can be withheld from a payment through [[tax]].
