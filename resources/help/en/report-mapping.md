## What it is

The financial statements group accounts into lines (*Cash and cash equivalents*, *Trade and other receivables*, *Cost of sales* …). This screen is where each account is given its line, and each balance sheet line its cash-flow class.

## When to use it

After adding accounts, or when you want the statements laid out differently.

## How to

- **Apply recommended mapping** maps a standard chart in one click (new companies are mapped automatically).
- Map a **group**: everything under it follows; an account can override its parent.
- Rename, reorder or add lines. The screen tells you how many accounts are still unmapped.
