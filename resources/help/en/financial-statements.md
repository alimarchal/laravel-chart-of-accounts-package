## What it is

One screen with three tabs: **Balance sheet**, **Income statement** and **Cash flow** (indirect method), laid out by statement lines (current assets, trade receivables, revenue, cost of sales …) rather than by account.

## When to use it

For reports you hand to others. Pick the date or period and, optionally, a **comparative** date or period; every line expands to the accounts behind it.

## How to

Check the layout under [[report-mapping]] first: accounts without a line appear on an "unmapped" line, so the statements still add up. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.

## Good to know

The cash-flow statement proves itself: opening cash plus the net change equals closing cash.
