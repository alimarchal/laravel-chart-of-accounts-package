## What it is

A **run** works out every employee's payslip for a month: basic salary, allowances, overtime, loan instalments, contributions and income tax. Then it posts the salary entry and, later, the payment.

## When to use it

Once a month. The same screen shows the year's totals (gross, tax withheld, net paid).

## How to

1. **New payroll run**: pick the month and click **Calculate**. Joiners and leavers are paid for the days they worked.
2. Open the run to check each payslip. **Recalculate** after changing anything (a draft can also be deleted).
3. If your company uses approval, HR clicks **Submit for approval** and finance **approves** it (never the person who submitted it).
4. **Post** books the salary entry. **Pay** clears the net salary payable against a bank or cash account. **E-mail payslips** sends each employee theirs; you can also print or download any payslip, and download the **bank salary file**.
5. **Void** reverses a posted run so the month can be run again.

## Good to know

- One run per month. A negative net salary is refused.
- The payroll tab strip at the top of every payroll screen takes you to employees, grades, loans, reports and the rest.
