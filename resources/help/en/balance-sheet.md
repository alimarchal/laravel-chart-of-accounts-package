## What it is

Assets on one side; liabilities and equity on the other. They must be equal, and the screen says whether they are.

## When to use it

For the owner, the bank and the auditor, at month end and year end.

## Good to know

- Profit of the current year that has not been closed yet is shown in equity, so the sheet always balances.
- For a layout with your own lines and a comparative date use [[financial-statements]].
