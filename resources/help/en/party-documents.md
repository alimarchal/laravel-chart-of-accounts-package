## What it is

The documents of the receivables and payables ledger: **invoices** and **credit notes** for customers, **bills** and **debit notes** for suppliers. Each has lines with an account, quantity, price and a tax code.

## When to use it

Whenever you sell on credit or receive a supplier's bill.

## How to

1. **New document**: pick the kind, the party, the dates and the lines. Tax is worked out from the tax code, with prices inclusive or exclusive of tax.
2. Save it as a **draft**; check it; **Post** it. Posting gives the document its gapless number (for example *INV-2026-00001*) and creates the journal entry.
3. To correct a posted document, **void** it (its entry is reversed) and issue a new one. A posted document is never edited.

## Good to know

- Receipts and payments are applied to documents on the [[party-payments]] screen; a document with payments applied cannot be voided until they are undone.
- A credit note is applied to invoices the same way.
- Posting to a closed period is refused.
