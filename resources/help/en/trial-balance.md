title: Trial balance
summary: Every account with its debit and credit totals; the two sides must be equal.
---
## What it is

The first check of any set of books: a list of all accounts with total debits, total credits and the balance. If the books are sound, **total debits equal total credits** and the screen says *Balanced*.

## When to use it

At month end before closing, whenever you doubt the books, and as a starting point for the other statements.

## How to

Use **Filters** to choose the date, the branch or a range of accounts. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.

## Good to know

Only posted entries are included.
