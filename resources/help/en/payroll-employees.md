## What it is

This group of screens defines pay: **Employees** (salary, dates, bank, cost center, whether tax is withheld), **Grades** (a basic salary with its allowances), **Pay items** (allowances and deductions, as a fixed amount, a percent of basic, or quantity × rate), **Contributions** (EOBI, PESSI, provident fund) and **Bulk changes**.

## When to use it

When someone joins, leaves, gets a raise, or when you introduce a new allowance.

## How to

- **New employee**: code, name, join date, monthly salary (or pick a grade), bank details; tick *Withhold income tax* and *Paid overtime* where they apply; choose the allowances and deductions.
- **Grades**: change a grade and everybody on it follows in the next payroll calculation. An employee's own values override the grade's.
- **Bulk changes**: give or take away an allowance for many people, move a group to a grade, or raise salaries by a percent or an amount; a **preview** is shown before anything is applied.
- A salary change keeps a history with the date it applies from and a reason; a change in the middle of a month pays each part at its rate.

## Good to know

Contribution rates and tax slabs are yours to set; check them against the current rules.
