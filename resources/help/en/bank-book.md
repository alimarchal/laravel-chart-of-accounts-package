## What it is

A report of the posted entries on your bank accounts: date, reference, description, money in and out, and the balance after each line.

## When to use it

To see what happened in the bank over a period, or to compare with the bank's own statement.

## How to

Use **Filters** to pick the bank account and the dates. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.
