## What it is

The list of things the company owns and uses for years (vehicles, furniture, equipment): cost, salvage value, useful life, depreciation method and the three accounts each asset posts to.

## When to use it

When you buy an asset, every month to book depreciation, and when you sell or scrap one.

## How to

- **New asset**: fill the cost, life and accounts. Give the account that paid for it (cash, bank or the supplier) and the purchase is booked for you.
- **Depreciation**: preview what is due up to a month end, then book it; one entry per month. Running it again does nothing, and missed months are caught up in order.
- **Dispose**: sell or scrap an asset; depreciation is brought up to date, the asset is removed and the gain or loss is booked.

## Good to know

- Cost, life and accounts are locked once the asset has entries.
- The register shows the difference between the ledger and the register; it should be zero.
