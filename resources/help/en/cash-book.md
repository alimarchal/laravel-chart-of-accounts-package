## What it is

The same report as [[bank-book]], for the accounts that play the *cash* role: money in, money out, closing balance.

## When to use it

For the daily cash count: compare the closing balance with the cash actually in the drawer.

## Good to know

Which accounts count as cash is set under Settings → [[account-roles]].
