## What it is

Stock records: **items** (with the accounts they post to), **warehouses**, and a ledger of **movements**. Value is kept at the moving average cost.

## When to use it

Whenever stock is received, issued, adjusted or moved.

## How to

- **Receive**: quantity, unit cost and the account that paid or owes. **Issue**: leaves at the average cost and books cost of goods sold. **Adjust**: a count difference. **Transfer**: between warehouses.
- The valuation shows quantity, average cost and value per item and warehouse at any date, flags items at or below their reorder level, and checks stock against the inventory accounts.

## Good to know

Stock never goes negative. An item's accounts are locked once it has movements, and items with movements can only be deactivated.
