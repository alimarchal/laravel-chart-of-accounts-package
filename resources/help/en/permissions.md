## What it is

All permissions of the accounting area, each named for what it allows (for example `journal-entries.post`, `payroll.run`, `periods.reopen`). Roles are made of them.

## When to use it

To find out which permission controls something, or to give one person an extra permission without changing their whole role (do this on the user's page).

## Good to know

Prefer changing roles to giving single permissions; it is easier to review later.
